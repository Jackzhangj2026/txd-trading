PP Hollow Board for Retail Displays and Signage: Applications and Benefits

Published: June 10, 2026 | Reading time: 5 minutes

Introduction

PP hollow board — widely known by the brand name Coroplast — has become one of the most versatile materials in the signage and retail display industry. From temporary point-of-purchase displays in supermarkets to long-term outdoor real estate signs and trade show exhibition booths, PP hollow board offers an exceptional balance of durability, lightweight construction, printability, and cost efficiency that traditional materials like cardboard, PVC foam board, and aluminum composite panels struggle to match.

The global retail signage and display market is projected to exceed USD 60 billion by 2028, and PP hollow board is capturing an increasing share of this segment. Importers and distributors across North America, Europe, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East are sourcing custom-printed PP sheets and fabricated display structures as retailers seek more sustainable, reusable, and economically viable alternatives to single-use cardboard displays. This article explores the key applications, material advantages, and sourcing considerations for PP hollow board in the signage and display industry.

Why PP Hollow Board Excels in Signage and Displays

Several intrinsic properties make PP hollow board the material of choice for short-term to medium-term signage and retail display applications.

Weather Resistance and Durability

Unlike corrugated cardboard, which loses structural integrity when exposed to moisture, PP hollow board is inherently waterproof. Outdoor yard signs, construction site hoardings, and event banners made from PP board withstand rain, snow, and high humidity without delaminating or softening. Standard PP grades offer reliable performance in temperatures from -10°C to 70°C, and UV-stabilized formulations extend outdoor service life to 12–24 months — significantly longer than paper-based alternatives. This weather resistance translates directly to reduced replacement costs and fewer customer complaints for sign distributors.

Lightweight Yet Rigid

A 4 mm PP hollow sheet weighs approximately 800 g per square meter — roughly one-third the weight of PVC foam board (Sintra/Komatex) and one-fifth the weight of 3 mm aluminum composite panel. This weight advantage reduces shipping costs substantially for international buyers. A 20-ft container can hold approximately 12,000 square meters of 4 mm PP board (flat-packed), compared to only 4,500 square meters of PVC foam board in the same container. For retail chains sourcing displays for hundreds of locations simultaneously, this freight efficiency translates to savings of thousands of dollars per shipment.

Printability and Customization

PP hollow board accepts a wide range of printing techniques. Screen printing produces vibrant, opaque colors ideal for spot-color brand identities. Digital UV printing enables full-color photographic graphics with variable data capabilities — perfect for retail displays that need localized messaging, QR codes, or seasonal promotions. For large-scale production runs, offset printing onto PP film that is then laminated to the board surface offers the highest quality at the lowest per-unit cost. The smooth outer surface of quality PP board provides excellent ink adhesion and color reproduction, rivaling the print quality of more expensive PVC and aluminium substrates.

Key Applications in Retail and Signage

Point-of-Purchase and Point-of-Sale Displays

PP hollow board dominates the temporary POP/POS display segment. Common configurations include countertop display units for cosmetics and electronics, floor-standing display shippers for packaged consumer goods, sidekick displays that attach to existing shelving, and dump bins for promotional merchandise. Major brands and retailers prefer PP board for seasonal and promotional displays because the material can be die-cut, scored, and folded into complex three-dimensional structures — enabling eye-catching display designs without the tooling costs associated with metal or acrylic fabrication. A well-designed PP display can support up to 25 kg of product while weighing less than 2 kg itself, making it easy to install without tools.

Real Estate and Construction Signage

Real estate signs — from residential "For Sale" posts to large-format commercial development hoardings — represent one of the largest volume applications for PP hollow board globally. The material's rigidity prevents wind flutter that plagues flexible vinyl banners, while its moisture resistance ensures professional appearance through rain and dew. PP board can be directly screen printed or fitted with digitally printed vinyl overlays. Common thicknesses range from 3 mm for small residential signs to 6 mm for large construction site signage and perimeter hoardings. Corrugated flute direction should be oriented vertically for ground-mounted signs to maximize bending stiffness against wind loads.

Trade Show and Exhibition Displays

The events and exhibitions industry demands displays that are portable, quick to assemble, visually impressive, and economical — all qualities where PP hollow board excels. Modular exhibition booth systems fabricated from die-cut PP panels can be flat-packed into a single flight case and assembled on-site without tools using tab-and-slot connections. For single-use trade show displays, printed PP boards offer a fraction of the cost of fabric or aluminum frame systems while delivering comparable visual impact. After the event, PP components can be recycled or repurposed, eliminating costly storage and logistics for reusable display inventory. As sustainability increasingly influences event procurement decisions, PP board's recyclability gives it a distinct advantage over PVC-based alternatives.

Yard Signs and Political Campaign Signage

The H-shaped yard sign — two stakes joined to a corrugated plastic face — is a ubiquitous application globally. Political campaigns, seasonal sales events, directional signage, and community announcements all rely on PP hollow board for this application. The standard 18×24 inch (457×610 mm) yard sign with 4 mm corrugated plastic has become an industry standard due to its optimal balance of visibility, wind resistance, and manufacturing economy. For political campaigns managing signs across thousands of locations, PP board's reusability across election cycles and its ability to be overstickered with updated messaging offer practical advantages over paper-based alternatives that must be fully replaced for each campaign wave.

Sourcing Considerations for Display and Signage Buyers

Material Grade Selection

Not all PP hollow board is created equal. Virgin-grade PP homopolymer offers the best surface finish, consistent thickness (±0.15 mm tolerance), and highest print quality — essential for premium retail displays where brand presentation matters. For cost-sensitive applications like construction hoardings or short-term event signage, recycled-content PP board (typically 30–70% post-industrial recycled material) offers adequate performance at 15–25% lower cost. However, recycled-grade board may have visible surface specks and less consistent flute geometry, which can affect printing results. Always request a pre-production print sample to verify quality meets your brand standards before committing to a full production run.

Thickness Selection Guide

Selecting the right board thickness for your specific application is critical:

  • 2–3 mm: Lightweight indoor signage, small shelf talkers, hanging tags. Suitable for self-supporting displays under 5 kg. Lowest cost and most compact for shipping.
  • 4 mm: The industry standard thickness. Ideal for yard signs, countertop POP displays, real estate signs, and medium-size trade show graphics. Supports loads up to 15 kg in well-designed display structures.
  • 5–6 mm: Heavy-duty floor-standing displays, construction hoardings, large-format outdoor signage. Provides superior wind resistance and supports loads exceeding 25 kg. Recommended for displays exceeding 1.2 m in height.
  • 8–10 mm: Structural exhibition components, modular booth walls, permanent wayfinding signage. Used when the board itself serves as a load-bearing element rather than just a graphic substrate.

Printing and Finishing Options

When sourcing from overseas suppliers, clarify the full range of post-processing capabilities. Die-cutting with precision steel-rule dies enables complex shapes with repeatable accuracy of ±0.5 mm. Scoring and creasing creates clean fold lines for 3D display structures — ensure the supplier uses heated scoring tools to prevent surface cracking on the fold. Corner rounding, hole punching, and edge sealing (using a flame or hot-knife process to create a smooth, sealed edge) are finishing details that significantly affect the professional appearance of the final product. For premium applications, specify lamination with a matte or gloss overlaminate to protect printed graphics from scuffing and UV fading, extending display life by 3–6 months.

Environmental and Economic Benefits

The shift from single-use cardboard and non-recyclable PVC to PP hollow board in the display industry is driven by both environmental and economic factors. A typical corrugated cardboard POP display used for an 8-week promotional campaign generates approximately 1.2 kg of waste that cannot be recycled if it carries plastic coatings or laminations. A comparable PP board display, by contrast, can be reused across multiple campaigns, reground into pellets at end of life, and re-extruded into new board — achieving near-zero material waste in a closed-loop system. Several major retailers now mandate recyclability as a procurement requirement for all temporary displays, giving PP board a decisive advantage over mixed-material alternatives.

From a cost perspective, while the per-unit purchase price of PP board is 20–40% higher than corrugated cardboard, the total cost of ownership favors PP board when considering durability, reusability, and reduced replacement frequency over a 12-month campaign cycle. Compared to PVC foam board, PP offers a 30–50% lower material cost plus significantly reduced freight expenses due to lighter weight, making it the most cost-effective rigid signage substrate for most volume applications.
